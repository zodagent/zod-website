Search & filters

One bar over chats, routines, pages, settings, memory, docs, help and every connector’s records. Words search like grep; filters narrow and act.

Open it

Press ⌘K or click the search box. Type to see results grouped by kind: chats and schedules, pages and tools, knowledge, and the open web. Results can be sorted by best, newest, oldest, A to Z or Z to A.

Words

Words are matched like grep, with a typo allowed. When the words find nothing, the linked model is asked what you meant and its phrasing is searched the same way. A plain question about Zod (“how do I change the wallpaper”) is answered from the docs and help, or opens the setting itself.

Filters

in:routinesgo to a place: routines, memory, agents, skills, plugins, connectors, settings, notifications, chats, files, shortcuts and any connector’s lists
is:error is:unreadnarrow by state
kind:…narrow by kind of row
after:30donly newer than a time
over:500a number filter, where a list has numbers
sort:recentorder the results
limit:20how many to show
match:exactliteral words only
| do:verbact on what was found, such as resume, read or submit

Press Tab to complete the filter you are typing, the arrow keys to move through results, Enter to open one, ⌘↵ to run a | do: chain, and Escape to clear and close.

The same language everywhere

What you type here is what a routine’s steps are made of and what the agent uses to find things. A search you like can be saved as a view.
