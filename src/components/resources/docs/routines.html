Routines & calendar

A routine is work Zod does for you, again and again. Zod makes it, you switch it on or off, and a routine with a script runs with no model, so a run costs no tokens.

What a routine is

A goal (what it is for), a script Zod worked out once, when it runs (by hand, once, every few minutes, daily, weekdays, weekly, or when something happens) and what it may do alone. Changes that need your OK are covered by grants you approve for that exact script; if the script changes, the grants stop applying until you give them again.

Explore, then Mine

Open Routines with in:routines or the sidebar. The month calendar sits at the top. Explore is ideas with a description each: one click opens a chat where Zod makes it. Mine lists your routines with a switch each, and the latest runs.

Ask for one

Say it in a chat, or type /routine, /setup or /learn: “every weekday at 8, tell me what needs me”, “set me up: connect my mail and summarise it every morning”, or paste a link to an agent, skill or workflow and Zod makes it a routine. A live panel opens beside the chat while Zod works, showing the goal, the script, what it may do alone and each call of the run.

What comes with Zod

Five scheduled routines: Morning briefing, Zod health check, Connector watch, Routine doctor and Weekly review. They only read, cost nothing and can be paused. Five run by hand with arguments: connect an MCP server, learn from a link, search everything, what is on today, vaults overview. What they find shows on Home as What’s new for you, each card with one thing to do.

When something fails

Zod writes a lesson and, if the script is at fault, a corrected one. Home shows a card with Apply it; nothing unattended changes without that click. A run cut off by a stop is reported, not repeated.

Call one by name

/its-name in any chat runs a routine, and @[Its title] hands one to Zod. Routines can call other routines: their changes join the caller’s plan and need the same grants.
