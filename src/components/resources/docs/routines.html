Routines & calendar

A routine is work Zod does for you, again and again. It says who does it, what it does and when it runs, and it shows its own calendar and runs.

One page for everything that runs on its own

Open Routines with ⌘4, in:routines or the sidebar. The page is a month calendar of what ran and what will, then your routines, their latest runs, your memory, and ready-made routines to start from.

Who

Zod, or one of your agents. Make or change an agent right on the routine’s page.

What

Words to ask, steps that run by themselves, or both.

When

By hand, once, every few minutes, daily, weekdays, weekly, or when something happens.

Where

Kept in Zod, or sent to a chat app such as Telegram or Slack.

Make a routine

1

Press New routine

A routine opens on a page of its own.

2

Choose who does it

Zod, or an agent such as Researcher. Pick “New agent” to make one without leaving.

3

Say what to do

Write it as you would ask in a chat. Add steps if you want parts to run without a model.

4

Choose when

The date and time pickers fit the window; a repeat can be as short as a few minutes.

5

Test, then save

Test the steps runs them without changing anything and shows what each step found.

Steps: the Zod language

One step per line. The same language is used by search, by routines and by the agent.

in:notifications is:errorfind rows: a place, then filters
| do:readact on what was found, with a verb
say: {{count}} unread errorssay something, using what was found
ask: shall I continue?stop and ask you
if … then … else …decide
skill: close-the-monthload a skill
agent: Researcherhand a part to an agent
read: memory:Invoice remindersbring a page in
run: another routinerun another set of steps
loop: …repeat

“How steps are written” on the routine’s page shows every kind of step with an example. Thirty-two ready-made step sets in seven groups are there to start from: research the web, code and projects, keep Zod healthy, memory and docs, skills and agents, connectors and tools, and decide and repeat.

Runs

  • Each run is saved with its result and shows in the Latest runs list.
  • A run cut off by a stop is reported as cut off and is not repeated.
  • You are notified when a run finishes or fails.
  • A routine’s run never takes over the window you are working in.

Parts of Routines

Memory, Agents, Skills and Plugins are opened from Routines, or by typing in:memory, in:agents, in:skills or in:plugins.
