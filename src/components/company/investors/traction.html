The releases, in order

Every one is on the changelog, with what changed.

  1. First beta

    Zod, local-first

    The desktop app: chat with tools, search, routines, memory, connectors, vaults, any OpenAI-compatible model.

  2. Chat

    Approvals, tool steps and answers you can act on

    One quiet card per tool step, Allow and Deny cards, and tables, charts and forms drawn in the chat.

  3. Vaults

    Each vault keeps its own look

    Separate workspaces, each with its own data, theme and wallpaper, and backups.

  4. Routines

    One page for everything Zod does on its own

    A month calendar, a Coming up list, routine pages with a dry run, and thirty-two ready-made routines.

  5. Extension 1.4

    All of Chrome, Chrome only

    The extension manages Chrome itself: tabs, windows, bookmarks, history and downloads, with a side panel and a Zod tab on every site.

  6. Beta 2 · Extension 1.6

    Home, vaults and context

    A simpler Home box with the model and the vault, the chat’s context on your terms, and no app on every new tab.
