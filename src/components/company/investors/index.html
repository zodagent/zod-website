Every slide, and what it answers

The sixteen slides a pitch deck needs, each answered for Zod on its own page.

  1. 01

    Cover

    A high-impact image, the company tagline, and the founder’s contact details.

  2. 02

    Problem

    A single, relatable pain point that motivated the business.

  3. 03

    Solution

    A clear, high-level overview of how the product addresses the problem.

  4. 04

    Market size

    Total addressable market, at least $1B, with clear growth potential.

  5. 05

    Competition

    A visual axis that shows the differentiation compared to others.

  6. 06

    Product

    Demos or clean screenshots that show the solution in action.

  7. 07

    Competitive advantages

    What makes the company unique and ahead of the curve.

  8. 08

    Features

    A high-level overview of key functionality. Optional, or in the appendix.

  9. 09

    Traction

    Data points such as user growth, revenue or testimonials that validate the market.

  10. 10

    Business model

    How the company makes money in a repeatable and scalable way.

  11. 11

    Revenue forecast

    A summarized three-to-five-year strategic roadmap, without raw spreadsheet screenshots.

  12. 12

    Raise

    The amount raised to date and the current fundraising target.

  13. 13

    Use of funds

    A clear breakdown of how the investment is allocated.

  14. 14

    Team

    Only the core leadership team.

  15. 15

    Advisors

    Key people who provide strategic guidance and social proof.

  16. 16

    Thank you

    A personalized final slide with team imagery and contact information.
