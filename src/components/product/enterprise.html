Enterprise & self-hosted. Coming soon.

Zod is local-first by design: there is no Zod cloud to sign up for and no data that has to leave your network. Running it on your own server for a team is coming soon. Today it runs as a desktop app and a Chrome extension on each person's computer.

Chats, routines, memory, connectors and settings are saved in files on the computer that runs Zod. Your only outbound traffic is the requests you send to the model and connectors you set up.

Data stays on your network

Point Zod at an internal OpenAI-compatible gateway or a local model server. Conversations never leave your network.

Connectors

Connect your own MCP servers and apps so agents can work with your internal systems, with a permission per tool: off, ask or allow.

Routines

Write repeatable work as routines with steps you can test with a dry run, so results are consistent.

Your own apps

Add an app connector with its own pages and tools for your internal services. Books, for accounting, is one.

Approvals

Anything that writes, deletes, installs or schedules asks first, and every run is recorded.
