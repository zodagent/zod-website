Data Use and Privacy Overview

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Local-first by default

Zod runs on your computer. There is no account and no Zod server that stores or processes your data. All workspace data lives on your device:

  • Chats, memory, routines, connectors, provider configuration and preferences are stored in files on your computer, in your vault’s data folder (by default ~/.zodagent)
  • Routines run while the app is running and keep each run’s result in the same files
  • We do not collect telemetry, analytics, or any usage data
  • You can back up your vault and restore it on another computer; a backup never contains keys or passwords

Requests to AI providers

The only data that leaves your device is what you explicitly send to an AI provider:

  • Requests go directly from your computer to the provider endpoint you configure (e.g. OpenRouter, OpenAI, Ollama, LM Studio) and to the connectors you add
  • We do not proxy, log, or inspect those requests
  • Your provider API key is stored on your computer and sent only to the provider address you linked
  • Anything you send to a provider is subject to that provider's own terms and data policies

Who can see your data

  • Zod: We cannot see your data. There is no server on our side to receive it.
  • Your AI provider: Only the specific content you send in a request, per their policy.
  • Your browser/device: Data persists locally until you delete the vault folder or uninstall the app together with its data.
  • Extension and desktop surfaces: The Chrome extension and desktop app act on your browser tabs and desktop only with your opt-in, and nothing is transmitted beyond the model and connector requests you trigger.
