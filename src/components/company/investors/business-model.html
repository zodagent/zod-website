Business model

Software you license. Inference you already pay for.

Zod does not run the model. Users bring their own, cloud or local, so Zod never pays for tokens and never has to pass that cost on. What we sell is the workspace: a per-seat license that renews, and support for teams that run it on their own server.

Why it is repeatable and scalable

Seats that renew

A per-seat license renews every year. Memory, routines and vaults make leaving expensive for the user and renewals likely.

No cost per token

The user pays their model vendor directly. Zod’s cost of goods is support and updates, not inference.

No cloud to scale

There is no Zod server. Ten thousand users cost about what ten do, because the app runs on their computers.

Later, a marketplace

Connectors, skills and routines made by others, sold through Zod with a revenue share. Books and its modules are the first.

How customers find us

The Chrome Web Store

The extension is the first thing most people install. It pairs with the desktop app by itself.

Demos

Teams book a demo on the site and get invited to the beta. The self-hosted edition starts from that list.

Students and communities

A students program, campus leads and ambassadors seed the next generation of users and connectors.
