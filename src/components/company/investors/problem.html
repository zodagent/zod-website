Problem

The work is on your computer. The assistant is not.

People paste their files, pages and numbers into a chat tab in someone else’s cloud, then copy the answer back by hand. An assistant that could act for them would need their files, their browser and their apps, and nobody wants to hand those to a server they do not control.

On screen · Tool steps

The fix: the agent reads your files and runs the tests on your computer, shows every step, and stops to ask before it deletes anything.

See how it works

“I want an assistant that can actually do things on my computer, without my data leaving it, and without one vendor deciding which model I use.”

Why today’s assistants fall short

One pain, three faces.

Copy, paste, repeat

The assistant cannot see your files or your browser, so every task starts with pasting context in and ends with pasting the answer out.

To act, it needs access

An agent that does the work needs your files, your browser and your apps. In a cloud assistant that means your data lives on a server you do not control.

One vendor, one model

Each assistant ties you to its own model and its own prices. Switching means losing your history, memory and setup.

Who feels it most

Professionals with sensitive data

Accountants, clinics, law offices and schools cannot paste client records into a public chat, so they get no assistant at all.

Developers and operators

They already run local models and tools, and want one workspace where an agent can use them safely.

Small teams

They want routines that run on their own, with a record of what happened, without buying a cloud platform.

What this slide answers

A single, relatable pain point that motivated the business.
