Competition

Local and acting. Most of the market is one or the other.

Cloud assistants can act, but on their servers. Local chat apps keep your data, but only talk. Coding agents act on your computer, but only on code. Zod is in the corner where few products are: it runs on your computer, acts with tools and routines across your files, browser and apps, and asks first.

On screen · Connectors

The corner Zod is in: it runs on your computer and acts through connectors, with a permission for every tool: off, ask or allow.
