Usage

Do

  • Use the wordmark and mark as provided, unmodified
  • Keep enough clear space around the mark
  • Call the product by its name: Zod Agent

Don't

  • Re-draw, rotate, or recolor the wordmark or mark
  • Place the mark on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Add suffixes like "Zod Agent Inc." without asking
