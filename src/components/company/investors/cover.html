Zod. One agent workspace on your computer.

A local-first agent that does the work and asks before it changes anything. A desktop app and a Chrome extension, any OpenAI-compatible model, no account, and your data in files on your computer. Zod is in beta, available by invitation.

On screen · Home

Home is a greeting and one prompt box. Click it and it opens to the model, the vault, four places and your last chats.

In three lines

Runs on your computer

Chats, routines, memory, connectors and settings are files in a vault you can back up or switch. There is no Zod cloud.

Asks before it changes anything

Looking asks nothing. Anything that writes, deletes, installs or schedules stops at an Allow and Deny card, and every run is recorded.

Any model

OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, Together, or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio. Whatever speaks the OpenAI API works.
