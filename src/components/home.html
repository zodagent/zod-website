Make it yours. One agent workspace on your computer that does the work, and asks before it changes anything.

Zod is a local-first app. Chat with an agent that uses real tools, search everything from one bar, set routines that run on their own, and let it remember what matters. It works with any OpenAI-compatible model . No account, and your data stays in files on your computer. Zod is in beta.

Built for real work.

Zod is one workspace for the work you repeat. Here is what it is for.

Research & briefings

Point the Researcher agent at a topic and get a short brief with sources. Make it a routine and it is waiting for you every morning.

See a routine

Coding partner

Choose a working folder. The agent reads, searches, edits and tests the project, and asks before it changes anything.

See how it asks

Routines that run themselves

Digests, stand-ups and reminders: by hand, once, every few minutes, daily, weekdays, weekly, or when something happens. A month calendar shows what ran and what will.

Your business apps

Connect your own apps with MCP. Books adds accounting, stock and payroll, with modules for clinics, schools, dispatch, legal and stays.

Your browser, tidied

With the Chrome extension, ask Zod to read a page, fill a form, group your tabs by topic or find that page you visited last week.

Private & local-first

Your data lives in files on your computer. Only the requests you choose go to the model and connectors you set up. There is no Zod account and no Zod server holding your work.

Any model. Your choice.

Link OpenAI, OpenRouter or a local server such as Ollama or LM Studio: anything that speaks /v1/chat/completions. Change the model from the chat at any time.

An agent that uses real tools

The agent calls nearly a hundred built-in tools and every tool your connected MCP servers offer, reads before it states a fact, and keeps going until the task is done. Anything that changes something waits for your yes.

Explore the tools

Answers you can read and act on

Zod draws tables, charts, figures, forms, lists and cards right in the chat, shows each tool step in one quiet card, and puts an Allow and Deny card in front of anything that changes something.

See a chat

Routines that actually run

Write a routine in plain words, as steps in the Zod language, or both. Test the steps with a dry run that changes nothing, then choose who does it and when . Each run is saved with its result.

Make a routine

Memory that stays yours

Say “remember that…” and Zod keeps it as a plain note you can read, edit and forget. Routines and chats find memories by their words.
