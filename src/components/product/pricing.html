How pricing works

Zod Agent runs entirely in your browser and connects to the model providers you choose. The app is free forever; you pay only the model providers you use.

The app is free

The workspace, tools, workflows, scheduler, and background agents are all free, forever.

Bring your own API key

Add keys for OpenAI, OpenRouter, Together, Groq, Ollama, LM Studio, or any OpenAI-compatible API.

You pay the provider

Model usage is billed by your provider, exactly as if you'd used their API directly.
