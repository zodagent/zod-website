Market size

A market already past a billion dollars, growing about 46% a year.

Agent software is one of the fastest-growing categories in software. Analysts put it at about $7.8 billion in 2025 and $52.6 billion by 2030. Zod goes after the part of that market that cannot, or will not, hand its files and apps to a cloud service.

On screen · Model

Why the market is open to a local app: the user links any OpenAI-compatible model, including one on their own computer, and Zod never pays for tokens.

Where Zod fits

From the whole market to what we go after first.

Total market

Every AI agent product: cloud assistants, automation platforms, coding agents and workspaces like Zod. Growing because models get cheaper and local models get good, so more work moves to agents that act.

Our segment

Knowledge workers and small teams who want an assistant that acts on their files, browser and apps, keeps data on their own machine, and lets them choose the model. Regulated fields first: accounting, clinics, law, schools.

What we win first

Individuals and small teams on the desktop app and the Chrome extension, by invitation during the beta. Then self-hosted teams on their own server, which is the next release.

Why it keeps growing

Inference is getting cheap

Every price cut by a model vendor lowers the cost of running an agent all day. Zod pays none of it; the user picks the model.

Local models are getting good

A laptop can now run a capable model. That makes a fully offline agent practical for the first time.

Privacy rules are getting stricter

Health, legal and financial work cannot go to a public chat. A workspace that keeps everything on the machine is the only one those users can adopt.

What this slide answers

Total addressable market, at least $1B, with clear growth potential.
