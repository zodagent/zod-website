Personal research agent

Ask the Researcher agent for a brief with sources, save what matters to Memory, and make it a routine so the brief is waiting each morning. Everything stays on your computer.

Coding helper

Point Zod at a local model (Ollama or LM Studio) and keep everything on your machine. Choose a working folder and the agent reads, edits and tests the project; the Allow and Deny card puts every change in front of you before it happens.

Routines with a calendar

Start a routine by hand, once, every few minutes, daily, weekdays, weekly or when something happens. Each run uses the full agent, tools included, and a month calendar shows what ran and what will.
