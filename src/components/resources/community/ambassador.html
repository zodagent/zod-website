Ambassador program

Help the community by hosting events, writing guides, and making sure people get help in the forum. No sales targets, no quotas.

What ambassadors do

Host events

Local meetups, study groups, or a live build session — we help you set it up.

Write guides

Blog posts, tutorials, and walkthroughs that get featured and linked from the docs.

Help in the forum

Answer questions and moderate discussions — the highest-value thing an ambassador can do.

Test previews

Get early access to new tools and features, and report what breaks.

Share the work

Showcase community builds on the channels and bring people into the forum.

Earn a badge

A verified ambassador badge on the forum profile for the work you do.

How it works

1

Apply in the forum

Tell us what you'd like to do — events, writing, or moderation. A short thread is all it takes.

2

Try something small

Run one office hour or write one guide. No commitment, no minimums.

3

Grow from there

Keep going as long as you enjoy it. The badge comes with the track record, not a contract.

Interested?

Start by telling us what you want to do. Applications live in the forum; for anything bigger, write to us directly.
